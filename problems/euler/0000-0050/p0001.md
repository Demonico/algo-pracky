# Multiples of 3 or 5

Problem 1

## Problem Statement

If we list all the natural numbers below 10 that are multiples of 3 or 5, we get 3, 5, 6 and 9. The sum of these
multiples is 23.

Find the sum of all the multiples of 3 or 5 below 1000.

## Explanation

The brute force approach works for this one. Save a result var initiated to 0. 
Iterate from 1 to the target value. If the current value is divisible by 3 or 5 
add it to the result i.e. cur mod 3 equals 0 or cur mod 5 equals 0.
